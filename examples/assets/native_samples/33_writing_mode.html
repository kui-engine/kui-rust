writing-mode

horizontal-tb (default)

writing-mode: horizontal-tb;
This is the default writing mode. Text flows horizontally from left to right, and blocks stack from top to bottom. Used by most Latin-based languages.

vertical-rl (East Asian traditional)

writing-mode: vertical-rl;
Vertical text flows from top to bottom, columns from right to left. Traditional CJK layout.
KUI Engine
Columns progress from right to left

vertical-lr

writing-mode: vertical-lr;
Vertical text flows from top to bottom, but columns progress from left to right. Used in Mongolian script.
