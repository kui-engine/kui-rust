Float & Clear

Float Left

float:left

Text wraps around the floated element. Float removes element from normal flow, positioning it left or right while surrounding content flows around it.

float: left Text wraps on right side

Float Right

float:right

Content flows to the left of the right-floated element. Commonly used for sidebar layouts and pull quotes.

float: right Text wraps on left
